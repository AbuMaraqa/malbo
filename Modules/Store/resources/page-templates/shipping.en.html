At {store} we want your order to reach you quickly and in perfect condition. This page explains how we prepare and deliver orders.

Where we deliver

We deliver to the areas our delivery team covers. If you are not sure about your area, contact us before ordering via {contact} and we will confirm.

Preparation and delivery times

  • Orders are usually prepared within one business day of confirmation.
  • Delivery usually takes one to three business days, depending on the area.
  • Holidays, busy seasons and unexpected circumstances can add time; we will tell you about any delay.

Delivery fees

Delivery fees depend on the area and are confirmed with you before the order is confirmed. We never add a fee without telling you.

Payment

You can pay cash on delivery, or by any other method we agree on when confirming your order.

On delivery

Please check your order when you receive it. If anything is damaged or missing, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we will sort it out right away.

Contact us

For any question about your order, contact us via {contact}.
