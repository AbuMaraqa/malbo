How do I order?

Choose the product, its options and the quantity, then tap "Order on WhatsApp". A chat with us opens with your order already written; send it and we will confirm the order and delivery with you.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, or another method we agree on when confirming your order.

When will my order arrive?

Usually within one to three business days, depending on your area. Details are in the shipping & delivery policy.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, before it leaves for delivery. Reach us as soon as you can via {contact}.

Can I exchange or return a product?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery under the returns & refunds policy.

The product I want is out of stock. What can I do?

Message us and we will tell you when it is back, or suggest a good alternative.

How can I reach you?

We are happy to hear from you via {contact}.
