Your satisfaction matters to us. If a product you bought from {store} is not right for you, you can exchange or return it under the terms below.

Time limit

You can request an exchange or a return within 7 days of receiving your order.

What we accept

  • The product is in its original condition, unused, with its packaging and tags.
  • It comes with proof of purchase (order number or confirmation message).

What cannot be returned

  • Products made or altered to order.
  • Perishable products, and personal-care products once opened, for hygiene reasons.
  • Discounted products marked as final sale at the time of purchase.

Damaged or wrong items

If a product arrives damaged or different from what you ordered, tell us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo. We will replace it or refund it in full, and we cover the delivery cost.

How to request a return

Contact us via {contact} with your order number and the reason, and we will arrange the pickup or drop-off with you.

Refunds

Once we receive and check the product, we refund the amount by the original payment method, or a method we agree on, within 7 business days. The original delivery fee is refunded only when the mistake was ours.
